# Saudi Trails

A bilingual (Arabic-first) map and list of public hiking trails in Saudi Arabia, under the Al-Barari brand, hosted on this site.

## Language

**Saudi Trails** (مسارات السعودية):
The trail map product itself.
_Avoid_: hiking trails page, trails artifact

**Al-Barari** (البراري):
The brand whose visual identity Saudi Trails wears.
_Avoid_: Barari blog, the identity

**Trail** (مسار):
One named, walkable hike published by a Source, with its length, elevation, duration and difficulty.
_Avoid_: track, hike, path

**Source** (الجهة):
The organisation that publishes a Trail: currently KSRNR or Darb.
_Avoid_: provider, publisher

**KSRNR**:
The King Salman bin Abdulaziz Royal Natural Reserve, a Source.
_Avoid_: the reserve, محمية (alone)

**Darb** (جمعية درب):
The Darb association, a Source.
_Avoid_: Darb app

**Start point** (نقطة البداية):
The published coordinates where a Trail begins.
_Avoid_: trailhead, location, pin

**Route** (خط المسار):
The full drawn line of a Trail, as opposed to its Start point.
_Avoid_: track, KML, path

**Shape** (نوع المسار):
How a Trail goes: a **Loop** (دائري) comes back to its Start point another way, an **Out-and-back** (ذهاب وعودة) comes back the same way, and a **One-way** (اتجاه واحد) Trail ends somewhere else. Read from the Route, or set by hand where there's no Route.
_Avoid_: circular, linear, return, round trip
